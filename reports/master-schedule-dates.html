Master Schedule Date Check

Compare Brightspace course dates with the master schedule, then write the schedule dates or a date you type.

How the dates should line up

Use this after the SIS drop, when Colleague will not push later date changes into Brightspace. Watermark reads the dates stored in D2L, so the fix is written here.

D2L stays open for 7 days after the master schedule end date.

The start date in D2L should match the schedule. The end date in D2L should be 7 days later. A course that ends on October 20 in the schedule should end on October 27 in D2L.

Upload a CSV with these columns: Term, Status, Short Title, CourseType, Start Date, End Date, and Combined Depts, Course Number, Section. Choose the Brightspace semester that matches the Term column, such as 26/FA.

Section offerings are included even when the Brightspace code ends in -COURSE, such as RT-121-FA700-26/FA-COURSE. CXLD courses, MERGED shells, and template shells with no section number are left out. Saving writes to Brightspace only. A date that already shows the right Eastern calendar day is left as it is. When another course in the semester already has the right dates, updated courses reuse that time of day. Otherwise the end date is saved as 11:59 p.m. Eastern so the course stays open through that day.
