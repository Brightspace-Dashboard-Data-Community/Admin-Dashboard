Recently enrolled, never logged in

Incremental check: students with an Enroll event in the selected window and no Brightspace system login in the last 31 days (June 2026 enrollment-log + tracking-log APIs). This is not the Data Hub lifetime Never Logged In extract, not a course-section merge, and not Office Hours Chat.

Enrollment logs are per user (/users/{id}/enrollmentlogs/). Tracking logs are system logins only, max 31 days (/users/{id}/usertrackinglogs/). Start with one course; a full semester scans every classlist then every student.
