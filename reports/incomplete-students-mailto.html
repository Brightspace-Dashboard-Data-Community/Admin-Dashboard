D2L Incomplete Student Role Change Tool

Change student roles and open emails in Outlook (or your default mail app) with one click.

How to Use This Tool

Same as the standard tool: use Tab 1 to mark a student Incomplete, Tab 2 to convert back to Student. After generating emails, use Open in Outlook to open your default mail client with the message pre-filled (no copy/paste). You can still copy to clipboard if needed.
