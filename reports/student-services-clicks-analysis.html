What this file records Each visit writes one page_view, then a click when someone hits a link, button, FAQ, or category toggle. There are no student names, emails, or D2L user IDs — only a random session id and user agent. Expanding a tile counts as a click even if the student never leaves the page. Production traffic is 12,768 of 12,911 events (99%); the rest is layout prototypes and local testing.

How usage lines up with the academic calendar

Tracking did not exist for most of Winter. It starts 31 March — week 12 of Winter 2026 (classes began 10 January, ended 1 May). Spring Summer is the only complete term in the file. Fall 2026 opened Saturday 29 August; this export covers the first four days, and 1 September is only a morning slice.

Volume over the 22 tracked weeks

Sessions stayed in a narrow band (~150–200/week) through late Winter, rose when Spring Summer opened in mid-May, dipped after Memorial Day, then climbed through July. The break is the week of 24 August (Fall Learning Days through Saturday class start): 984 sessions and 1,258 clicks — more than double any prior week. The week of 31 August is incomplete (Mon–Tue morning only) but already has 423 sessions.

Fall launch is a different product moment Monday 31 August is the single largest day in the file: 838 events from 391 sessions. Saturday 29 August (official class start) already had 136 sessions. If Fall holds even half of this daily rate, the hub will see more traffic in one Fall month than in all of tracked Winter. Staffing, content accuracy, and broken-link checks matter most in the 10 days around Learning Days through week one.

What people actually do on the page

Clicks are not all outbound. 60% are expand/collapse on support and resource tiles. Students browse the catalog, then a smaller set follow a “Go to …” action. That still produced 2,581 trips to other Delta sites.

Median session is a single page view and nothing else. Mean is 2.2 events. 90th percentile is 4 events; the longest session is 54. About 2,365 of 5,911 sessions (40%) interact. Campus Resources tiles are opened nearly twice as often as Direct Support (2,804 vs 1,413 category toggles).

What the data is telling us

  1. The hub is a registration-and-navigation tool more than a “while I’m in class” page. Spikes sit on term boundaries, grades due, Learning Days, and Fall opening — not mid-semester Mondays in April.
  2. The primary student journey is Advising → Self-Service → Registration/Aid → D2L/Online Learning → Library. If those five links are wrong, slow, or mislabeled, that is where the pain shows up.
  3. Most visits are shallow. Design for scanability: clear tile titles, a working search box, and correct “Go to” URLs beat adding more FAQ copy. Sixty percent never click; they may still have gotten the answer from the tile label.
  4. Fall week one is the stress test. 391 sessions on the first Monday is not an anomaly next to Learning Days traffic the week before. Plan content freezes and link QA for the 24 Aug–7 Sep window every year.
  5. Basic-needs and access links (food pantry, coordinated care, transportation, password reset, disability FAQ) are small in count and high in importance. They should stay visible, not buried behind the academic tiles that dominate volume.
  6. Winter 2026 cannot be compared fairly to Spring Summer or Fall. Only the last month of Winter is in the database. Use Spring Summer 2026 as the baseline term, and treat Fall 2026 as a launch curve until more weeks accumulate.

Methodology and caveats

Session id is created in the browser on page load. A refresh or a new device is a new session, so 5,911 is visits, not unique students. Element text is the first 200 characters of the clicked node, which is why some labels concatenate; destinations were rolled up by URL. Term assignment uses calendar start/end dates inclusive, in Eastern local date of created_at. Events between Winter end (1 May) and Spring start (11 May), and between SS end (20 Aug) and Fall start (29 Aug), are labeled as breaks. 1 September 2026 is truncated at 8:30 a.m. Eastern, so Fall “per day” (425) is pulled up by 29–31 August and will change when a full week exists.
