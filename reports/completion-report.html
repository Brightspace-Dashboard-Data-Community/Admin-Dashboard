Completion Report

Track learner progress-to-completion for courses in the selected semester using final grades, content progress, and optional awards.

Filters & Actions

What this pulls:
• Final grade for each learner (used for pass/complete flag).
• Content progress from module/topic completion to show % complete.
• Optional award/certificate if your course issues one.
Define your institution's completion rule in the JS (e.g., Passing Grade OR Content ≥ 90% OR Award earned).
